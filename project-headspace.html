Sleep Mood – Headspace App Feature Concept

Overview:

Sleep Mood is a feature concept designed for the Headspace app to help users relax and prepare for sleep. The idea was to create a calming nighttime experience that supports better sleep habits through relaxing content and a softer interface.

This was a group UX/UI project completed with two other designers. Together, we explored how Headspace could introduce a sleep-focused mode that reduces stimulation and helps users wind down before bed.

Solution:

We designed a Sleep Mood toggle that transforms the interface into a more calming nighttime experience.

When activated:

* The app switches to a cooler, darker color palette

* Brightness and stimulation are reduced

* Sleep-focused content becomes easier to access

Users can explore relaxing features such as soundscapes, bedtime stories, informative videos, tips, and inspirational quotes.

My Contributions

For this project, I designed three sections of the Sleep Mood experience:

Informative Videos:

A library of short videos that explain mindfulness, stress management, and mental wellness.

Tips:

Quick, actionable advice that helps users practice mindfulness and build healthier habits.

Quotes:

A collection of inspirational quotes users can browse and save to their personal library.
