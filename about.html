  • About Me

    Hi! I’m Salma, a UX/UI designer based in Vancouver, BC. I enjoy creating thoughtful digital experiences that are simple, clear, and visually engaging. Most of my work focuses on UX/UI and branding, and I love taking ideas from early sketches, wireframes, and all the way to polished designs in Figma. I’m always curious about how people interact with products and how design can make those experiences feel easier and more enjoyable.
